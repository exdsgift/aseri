# Code of conduct

aseri is a small project about forecasting. Everyone is welcome in its issues, pull requests and discussions, and the same few rules apply to all of them.

- **Be kind and be specific.** Criticise the work, a forecast or a method, never the person. Say what is wrong and what you expected.
- **Assume good faith, and say when you are unsure.** Most problems here are a misread source or a missing detail, not bad intent.
- **No harassment.** No insults, personal attacks, discrimination, sexualised content, doxxing or repeated unwanted contact.
- **Keep it on topic.** The series cover wars and shipping routes. Discuss them as data and forecasts. Do not use the project to promote violence or to attack a people, a country or a group.
- **Do not read the forecasts as advice.** aseri is for awareness, not decision support.

## Enforcement

The owner moderates the project. Comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules can be edited, hidden or closed, and repeated or serious breaches lead to a block. To report one, use GitHub's "Report content" on the comment or [open a private security advisory](https://github.com/exdsgift/aseri/security/advisories/new) and say it is a conduct report.

This text is the project's own and short on purpose. It is in the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
