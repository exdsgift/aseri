# Covariate scout, 2026-11-04

The monthly re-test of candidate covariates (spec 6.5, `pipeline/aseri/scout.toml`). The scout proposes and a human decides: the daily forecasts stay as they are until a proposal is approved.

**Passed: none.** The daily forecasts stay univariate.

- **Model:** `aseri-timestral209m_c1`, the production forecast with its calibration offsets, made without and with each candidate's rows on the same origins.
- **Data:** the archive's newest vintage, of 2026-11-03, read-only. Pseudo-real-time: each context is that vintage cut at its origin; each past-only covariate is held back by its publication lag (its `expected_lag` in series.py less the target's `fewest_lag` in the config); each calendar row is a function of the date alone.
- **Origins:** up to 26 a series, weekly on its record weekday, the newest whose every step is observed and settled, its `score_lag` past, by the series' own vintage.
- **Keep rule,** in every bucket the series has: WQL on the 21 levels at least 2% better, with its 90% interval below 0 (a stationary block bootstrap over origins, mean block 8); MASE no worse; and the 8 in 10 range's coverage inside the binomial acceptance region at 5% for n = the bucket's origins.
- **Runtime:** 728 forecasts made, 0 from the cache, 1627 s of model time.

## Caveats

- **A pass is a proposal, not a finding.** Each month re-tests mostly the same origins, so a candidate gets many chances to pass by luck. Confirm it on origins it has not been tested on before enabling it.
- **Pseudo-real-time.** The archive holds real vintages only from 2026-10-30; older contexts are today's values, which a revising source (GPR, PortWatch) has since changed.
- **Training data.** Timestral's training cutoff is unknown, so older origins may be in it.
- **Enabling** a candidate is a human decision under `[enabled]` in scout.toml. The series then gets a model ID of its own (`scout.model_id`), and its live record restarts.
